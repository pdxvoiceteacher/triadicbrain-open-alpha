# Output guide

**[IMPLEMENTED] Artifact roles · [PROPOSED] Human interpretation remains case-specific**

The demo output is a deterministic contract-fixture review set, not a single
answer file. Its manifest is the controlling inventory for exact filenames and
digests in that run. The Sophia- and Atlas-labeled files demonstrate artifact
roles; they are not outputs from invoking the inherited implementations.

| Artifact family | What it records | What it does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task and request envelope | The requested operation, source scope, consent, and identifiers | That the request is wise, safe, or authorized beyond its fields |
| Source and grounding bundle | Accepted source bytes, normalization, evidence references, and digests | That the source is true or complete |
| Candidate packet | The fixed fixture proposal and its declared evidence link | That the candidate is an answer or that every claim is true |
| Sophia-labeled fixture audit | The demo's fixed non-rewrite audit contract | An executed inherited Sophia audit or permission to accept the candidate |
| Atlas-labeled fixture posture | The demo's fixed bounded-orientation contract | An executed inherited Atlas posture, canonization, publication, or action authority |
| Human review | The allowed choices and the fixture's controlling `PENDING` state | A submitted decision or broader release rights |
| Run manifest and `SHA256SUMS.txt` | Output membership, byte identities, and closure | Semantic correctness, safety, or legal clearance |

## Reading failures and holds

A failure receipt is a valid output when a boundary cannot be satisfied. Do not
delete it and continue from a later artifact as if the failed gate passed. A
missing artifact, skipped required test, mismatch, or unresolved human decision
remains a hold.

## Comparing two fixture runs

Run the demo into two clean directories. Compare `source.txt`, the six JSON
review artifacts, `run_manifest.json`, and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Environment-specific
logs or outer filesystem metadata may be reported separately; they must not be
smuggled into the set claimed byte-identical.

## Privacy

The included fixture is synthetic and intended for offline use. Do not replace
it with credentials, private records, personal source documents, or material you
are not authorized to review. Review exports before sharing them, even inside a
private workflow.
