# Your first review

**[IMPLEMENTED] A bounded review sequence**  
**Repository source: PUBLIC DEVELOPMENT · Formal release: NONE · Rights: HOLD · Authority effect: NONE**

The included demo is deliberately small and deterministic. It demonstrates the
artifact contract without invoking the inherited component route. Its purpose
is to make the review boundary inspectable, not to demonstrate full integration,
general intelligence, or live-provider quality.

## 1. Read the task and source

Begin with the request envelope and the exact source bytes. Confirm that the
task, source scope, and consent fields match what you intended. Stop if they do
not.

## 2. Inspect the grounding bundle

The grounding bundle is the evidence substrate. It binds the accepted source
bytes, normalized references, and digests used later in the route. Confirm that
the candidate's evidence references point back to material actually present in
this bundle.

## 3. Treat the candidate as a proposal

The demo candidate packet contains fixed synthetic fixture text, a structure,
and an evidence link. It is not an answer, fact, instruction, or authorization.
Check each material claim against its cited source segment and note omissions or
unsupported language.

## 4. Read Sophia's audit separately

In the architecture, Sophia evaluates immutable candidate and route artifacts
without generating or rewriting them. The root demo emits a fixed
Sophia-labeled fixture artifact; it does not execute the inherited Sophia audit.
Use it to inspect the non-rewrite contract, not as live audit evidence.

## 5. Read Atlas as orientation

In the architecture, Atlas assigns a bounded posture to help a reviewer find
holds and next checks. The root demo emits a fixed Atlas-labeled fixture posture;
it does not execute the inherited Atlas implementation. Atlas cannot canonize,
publish, deploy, write memory, or increase authority. A friendly presentation
is not a correctness guarantee.

## 6. Preserve the human decision boundary

The fixture lists the bounded choices approve, hold, reject, or request repair,
but the root public-development page is intentionally read-only and leaves the decision
`PENDING`. If a later authorized workflow records a person's choice and reason,
keep that record separate and checksum-bound. It must not be overwritten by
Sonya/Coherence, Sophia, Atlas, or a replay.

## 7. Verify the checksum-closed export

Use the manifest and checksums to confirm that the reviewed files are the files
being returned. A successful checksum proves byte identity within the declared
package; it does not prove truth, fitness, safety, rights, or permission to
publish.

For file families and cautions, see the [output guide](output-guide.md). For
non-authorized actions, see [safety and boundaries](safety-and-boundaries.md).
